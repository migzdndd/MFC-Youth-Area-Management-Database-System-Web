# Design Direction — MFC Youth AMS

> **Official Brand & UI Direction for Missionary Families for Christ (MFC) Youth Area Management System**
> Loaded by `antislop`, `ui-ux-pro-max`, and frontend design agents.

## Identity & Soul
- **Product Name:** Missionary Families for Christ (MFC) Youth Area Management System (`MFC Youth AMS`)
- **Tone:** Reverent utility, high community trust, pastoral dignity, field-ready speed.
- **Soul:** An uncluttered servant leadership portal built specifically for pastoral care, household monitoring, event verification, and ministry stewardship for Catholic youth (ages 13 to 21).

## Dials
- **Dial:** `ENERGY 1 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1`
- **Variance:** `4/10` (Balanced / Modern)
- **Density:** `7/10` (Field Operational Density)
- **Motion:** `2/10` (Subtle 150–200ms ease)

## Color Palette
- **Primary:** Deep Community Navy (`#002847`)
- **Navy Shades:** Navy Deep (`#00182C`), Navy Light (`#0A3D66`), Navy Surface (`#06233B`)
- **Canvas / Background:** Crisp Off-White (`#F8FAFC`)
- **Surface:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF`)
- **Text Tokens:** Text Main (`#0F172A`), Text Muted (`#475569`), Text Subtle (`#64748B`)
- **Community Accent:** Dignified Warm Gold / Amber (`#D97706`), Gold Light (`#FEF3C7`), Gold Dark (`#B45309`)
- **Status Tokens (WCAG AA Compliant):**
  - Green / Success: `#15803D` text on `#DCFCE7` background
  - Amber / Warning: `#B45309` text on `#FEF3C7` background
  - Red / Danger: `#B91C1C` text on `#FEE2E2` background
  - Blue / Info: `#0284C7` text on `#E0F2FE` background

## Typography
- **Headings:** `Poppins`, sans-serif (Weights 600, 700)
- **Body & Continuous Copy:** `Open Sans`, sans-serif (Weights 400, 500, 600)
- **UI Labels & Controls:** `Raleway`, sans-serif (Weights 600, 700)

## Craftsmanship & Mobile Constraints
- **Minimum Tap Target:** 44px × 44px on all touch surfaces (`min-h-[44px]`).
- **Focus Rings:** Distinct `:focus-visible` outline (`2px solid #002847`, `offset 2px`).
- **Responsive Website:** Desktop sidebar navigation, accessible mobile drawer (< 1024px), skip-to-content accessibility link, zero horizontal scroll leak, and responsive card lists for tables.
- **Zero Slop:** No generic AI blue-purple gradients, no glowing borders, no pill-shaped cards/inputs, no fake metrics or testimonials, no em dashes (`—`) in UI copy.
