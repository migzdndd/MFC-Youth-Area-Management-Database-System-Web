### 1. Purpose and Operational Character of Financial Modules

The financial modules of the MFC Youth Area Management System Web Platform (`mfc-youth-area-management-system.vercel.app`, hereinafter the "Platform") exist solely as an **internal administrative ledger and accounting registry**.

The Platform serves to provide transparency, accountability, and accurate record-keeping for community servant leaders and couple coordinators managing community resources in Missionary Families for Christ (MFC) Youth - NCR Central.

---

### 2. Voluntary Charitable Contributions: God Is Good (GIG)

1. **Nature of GIG Offerings:** In Missionary Families for Christ, "God Is Good" (GIG) contributions are voluntary, freewill Christian offerings given by members, leaders, and supporters to support the ongoing spiritual mission, pastoral training, evangelization camps, and operational needs of the youth ministry.
2. **Voluntary and Non-Compulsory:** Participation in GIG offerings is completely voluntary. No member is ever coerced or required to give a contribution as a condition of spiritual fellowship or membership in the community.
3. **Non-Refundable Charitable Donations:** Because GIG contributions represent completed charitable offerings dedicated immediately to community evangelization and ministry work, all GIG contributions are non-refundable once given.
4. **No Commercial Consideration:** GIG contributions do not constitute payment for commercial goods, contractual services, or tangible consumer products. No commercial invoices or VAT tax receipts are generated by the Platform.

---

### 3. Event and Youth Camp Registration Fees

1. **Operational Cost Recovery:** Registration fees collected for entry Youth Camps, Kids Camps, Youth Conferences, Servant Team Retreats, and leadership assemblies are calculated strictly on a cost-recovery basis. These fees cover direct operational disbursements, including:
   * Retreat center lodging and facility rental;
   * Meals, snacks, and drinking water for participants;
   * Camp kits, prayer booklets, identification tags, and event shirts;
   * Sound system, liturgical supplies, and logistical transport.
2. **Subsidies and Youth Sponsorships:** Where financial hardships exist, MFC Youth operates a pastoral subsidy policy supported by community tithers to ensure no youth is denied participation in a Youth Camp due to financial inability.
3. **Refund and Cancellation Guidelines:** Event fees collected externally may only be refunded or reallocated to future camps in accordance with the specific pastoral policy of the organizing Area or Sector. Inquiries regarding refunds must be addressed directly to your Chapter Couple Coordinator.

---

### 4. External and Offline Payment Processing Disclosure

1. **No Embedded Payment Processing:** The Platform does **not** process, authorize, or settle monetary transactions. It does not integrate payment processor SDKs (such as credit card payment gateways, Stripe, or PayPal merchant checkouts) on the web domain.
2. **Accepted External Channels:** All community contributions and event fees are remitted through external channels:
   * Physical cash handed directly to designated Chapter Finance Servants or Couple Coordinators during household meetings or camp check-in;
   * Direct electronic fund transfers via GCash or Maya to verified community servant accounts;
   * Direct bank deposit or online fund transfer (e.g., BPI, BDO, UnionBank) to official community custody accounts.
3. **Ledger Role Only:** When a servant leader records a payment on the Platform, they are entering an administrative confirmation (recording the amount, transaction date, and external payment reference code or receipt number). The Platform acts strictly as an informational audit trail.
4. **No Financial Custody:** The Platform does not hold, custody, or transmit money, nor does it function as an Electronic Money Issuer (EMI), remittance agent, or banking institution under the regulations of the Bangko Sentral ng Pilipinas (BSP).
