# Parental and Legal Guardian Consent Notice
## Minor Protection Addendum for Parents and Guardians

**Organization:** Missionary Families for Christ (MFC) Youth / MFC Kids - NCR Central  
**Platform URL:** `https://mfc-youth-area-management-system.vercel.app`  
**Statutory Framework:** Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012), The Family Code of the Philippines, and National Privacy Commission Guidelines on Children's Data

---

### A Letter to Our MFC Parents and Guardians

Dear Parents and Guardians in Christ,

Peace be with you.

In Missionary Families for Christ, we believe that the family is the domestic church and our most sacred ministry. When your child participates in MFC Kids (ages 4 to 12) or MFC Youth (ages 13 to 21), you entrust us not only with their spiritual growth and friendship but also with their physical safety and personal privacy.

The MFC Youth Area Management System Web Platform is a secure, private administrative tool used by our community's servant leaders, couple coordinators, and youth ministers to organize Youth Camps, maintain chapter rosters, ensure safety during retreats, and coordinate pastoral care.

Because your child is under the age of 18, Philippine law and our community standards require us to obtain your clear, informed consent before collecting, storing, or processing any of their personal details. This document explains what information we collect, why we need it, who can see it, and how you retain total control over your child's records at all times.

---

### 1. What Information Do We Collect About Your Child?

When you register your child for an MFC Youth Camp, Kids Camp, or chapter household, we collect only the minimum necessary information required for their safety and pastoral care:

* **Basic Identity:** Your child's full legal name, preferred nickname, gender, and date of birth.
* **Contact Information:** Mobile phone number and email address (if your teenager has their own; otherwise, only parent contact details are recorded).
* **Residential Details:** Home address (used solely to assign your child to the nearest local parish or community chapter).
* **Family and Emergency Contacts (Mandatory):** Parent or legal guardian full names, active mobile numbers, and home address.
* **Camp and Activity History:** The year and batch of the Youth Camp or Kids Camp your child attended, assigned household group, and attendance at community gatherings.
* **Special Pastoral or Medical Notes:** For overnight camps, any allergies, existing medical conditions, or dietary restrictions provided on camp registration forms to ensure their physical well-being.

---

### 2. Why Do We Collect This Data and How Is It Used?

Your child's information is used solely for the following non-commercial pastoral purposes:

1. **Safety and Emergency Preparedness:** Having accurate parent contact numbers and medical notes allows our couple coordinators and medical team to reach you immediately in case of illness, injury, or urgent camp situations.
2. **Age-Appropriate Grouping:** Ensuring your child is placed in the proper ministry program (MFC Kids for children under 13; MFC Youth for teenagers and young adults) and assigned to age-matched household discussion groups.
3. **Pastoral Accompaniment:** Helping youth leaders and couple coordinators support your child's spiritual growth, track retreat attendance, and communicate details of upcoming community gatherings.
4. **Community Administration:** Managing attendance records, service team assignments, and tracking graduation milestones as your child grows in the community.

---

### 3. Strict Safeguards: Who Can See Your Child's Information?

We treat minor data with the highest level of confidentiality and restriction:

* **No Public Profiles:** Your child's name, photos, phone numbers, and addresses are **never** displayed on a public website or made searchable on the internet.
* **No Social Media Selling or Ad Tracking:** We never share, sell, or disclose your child's information to commercial companies, advertisers, marketing agencies, or outside organizations.
* **Strict Role-Based Access:** Only authorized, screened servant leaders and adult Couple Coordinators who have direct pastoral responsibility over your child's specific Chapter or Sector can view their record.
* **Database-Level Protection:** Our database enforces PostgreSQL Row Level Security (RLS). A servant leader in Quezon City, for instance, cannot view or access the records of youth in Manila or Pasig.
* **No Automatic Online Account for Minors:** Registering your child in our community directory does not create an online login account. Your child is not given web credentials to access the administrative system unless they are an accredited, trained youth servant serving under adult supervision.

---

### 4. Your Rights as a Parent or Legal Guardian

Under Republic Act No. 10173 (Data Privacy Act of 2012) and community policy, you maintain permanent authority over your child's personal data. You have the right to:

1. **Review Your Child's Record:** You may at any time request to see the exact personal information stored about your child in our system.
2. **Correct Inaccuracies:** You may request updates or corrections to contact numbers, addresses, or medical notes at any time.
3. **Revoke Consent and Mandate Deletion:** You may withdraw your consent at any time. Upon your written or email request, we will promptly and permanently delete your child's record from our active database.
4. **Limit Communications:** You may specify that community announcements and camp invitations be sent only to your email or phone number rather than directly to your child.

---

If you wish to review your child's profile, update contact information, or request the immediate removal of their data from the MFC Youth Area Management System, please contact our Area Data Protection Officer and Couple Coordinators through any of the following channels:

### 6. Summary of Parental Acknowledgment

When you sign a physical youth camp waiver, fill out an MFC Kids registration slip, or submit an official online camp form, you formally acknowledge that:

* You have read and understood this Parental Consent Notice and the community Privacy Policy;
* You grant permission to MFC Youth / MFC Kids - NCR Central to record and process your child's personal and emergency contact details for community pastoral care and camp safety;
* You understand that consent may be revoked by you at any time without penalty or loss of fellowship in community gatherings.
