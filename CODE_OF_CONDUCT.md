# Code of Conduct

This Code of Conduct applies to contributors, reviewers, and collaborators participating in the MFC Youth Area Management System demonstration project.

## Our Standards
- Be respectful, empathetic, and kind to others.
- Give and gracefully accept constructive feedback.
- Harassment, trolling, and inappropriate language are strictly prohibited.

## Enforcement
Instances of unacceptable behavior can be reported privately to **miguel7riovaldez@gmail.com**. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly by the maintainers.
